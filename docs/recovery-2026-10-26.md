## Data contract

`/api/companies/snapshot.json` is a statically exported, machine-readable evidence view. Every company is unique. Missing or unverified price/weekly return/valuation/calendar is JSON null. Private companies have no public quote or earnings calendar. Valuations require an independently recorded source and date; a successful quote refresh does not redatestamp a legacy valuation. UI financial parameters suppress unsourced legacy values as well. Per-card source links expose quote and valuation dates.

The historical relevance/purity/elasticity scores, estimated market caps and colour weights remain research inputs, **not audited revenue exposure, measured revenue elasticity, or a trading recommendation**. The UI explicitly labels this distinction; measured revenue elasticity is always null in the evidence JSON. Prices are delayed provider observations in listing currency. Weekly change is five daily close intervals (not calendar-week or total return); unavailable reference history stays unknown.
